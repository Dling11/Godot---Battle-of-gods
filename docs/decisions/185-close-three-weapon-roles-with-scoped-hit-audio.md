# Decision185: Close three weapon roles with scoped contact audio

2026-10-06. Status: implemented initial closure; owner feel/campaign balance pending.

The owner accepts the axe motion and requests clearer weapon icons, descriptions, actual damage/DPS distinctions and less repetitive metallic monster hits. They explicitly freeze Greatsword and later authorize online audio resources.

Decision: preserve all accepted motion, give sword/axe/dual distinct normal-hit rolls with unchanged25 Skill Power, derive Bag combo/DPS stats from runtime tuning, compose truthful icons from approved steel, and use one shared licensed body-impact layer with family pitch/gain. Only these three opt in. Actual contact/deduplication remains combat authority; presentation does not infer damage from two blades or huge slashes.

Alternatives: one identical metallic cue for all families remained repetitive; one new sound/art set per enemy or weapon variant adds needless content before core feel is accepted; globally replacing the old file would alter frozen Greatsword. Separate optional hit profiles avoid those consequences. Existing air swings remain distinct from accepted body impacts.

Consequences: stronger axe contacts, faster lighter dual exchange, clear comparison math and reusable sound setup for future variants. Null audio/readout defaults preserve current Greatsword and other equipment. This does not introduce scythe, crafting recipes or acquisition stages. Effective encounter DPS and subjective sound mix still need owner review. [Current numbers, source/license, files and proof](../design/king-weapon-closure.md).
