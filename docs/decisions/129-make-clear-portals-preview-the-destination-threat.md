# Decision 129: Make Clear Portals Preview the Destination Threat

## Status

Accepted — 2026-08-24

## Context

Portal tiers had drifted between two meanings: the danger of the stage just defeated and the danger waiting beyond the portal. That made Stage III's exit remain purple after its mini-boss was already dead, and Stage V produced another red Boss portal even though Varkuun had been defeated. Stage V also still returned to Sanctuary, preventing the implemented campaign chain from naturally continuing into Stage VI.

## Alternatives Considered

1. Color the portal from the encounter just completed.
2. Keep every direct stage transition blue and reserve threat colors for Sanctuary previews.
3. Color each stage-clear portal from the destination's threat and reset to Normal after a mini-boss or boss when the next route is ordinary.

## Decision

Choose option 3. A stage-clear portal communicates what waits on the other side:

- Stage I to II: Normal, blue, no lightning.
- Stage II to III: Mini Boss, purple, restrained lightning.
- Stage III to IV: Normal, blue, no lightning.
- Stage IV to V: Boss, red, strong lightning.
- Stage V to VI: Normal, blue, no lightning.
- Stage VI to Sanctuary: Normal, blue, no lightning, until Stage VII receives an implemented destination contract.

Varkuun's final dialogue, collapse, milestone chest claim, progression recording, and safe-point save remain mandatory and retain their existing order. Only after the successful claim does Stage V create the Normal portal directly into Stage VI. Returning to Sanctuary through the pause/defeat flow remains separately available and does not redefine the clear route.

## Consequences

- Purple and red warn about upcoming superiority instead of lingering after that threat has already been defeated.
- The implemented Forest campaign now advances continuously from Stage I through Stage VI.
- Stage V reward safety remains unchanged; entering Stage VI cannot bypass Varkuun's chest claim.
- Stage VII can replace Stage VI's temporary Sanctuary destination once its encounter and threat tier are approved.
