# Decision 135: Make Divine Descent the Examiner Phase Transition

## Status

Accepted and implemented in the debug F7 proof — 2026-08-25. Production Stage VII outcomes, rewards, replay, and final numerical balance remain open.

## Context

Axiom Divide already gives the Examiner a precise high-tier technique, but the fight still needed one unmistakable phase event, a reason for the Court's four pylons to exist, and a fair first lesson in divine-scale arena mechanics. The event also had to preserve the power ladder: the Examiner is a Disciple using controlled technique, not a god rewriting reality.

## Decision

- **Divine Descent** is the Examiner's official Phase I-to-II transition. Axiom Divide remains a repeatable signature combat technique rather than the transition itself.
- The F7 proof triggers at 70% health or through an Examiner-only debug button.
- Combat stops, King is briefly locked, the camera focuses the Examiner, the music ducks, and the Examiner calmly says: `Interesting.` / `Then let us continue.`
- Fresh whole-body frames communicate compression, launch, fall, impact, and recovery. Root movement only carries those authored poses through space; it never substitutes a translated idle.
- Once the Examiner leaves the arena, control returns for a 3.8-second cover window. A white-gold multi-layer seal charges at the arena center while four cyan pylon zones expose the exact protection geometry.
- The impact deals 260 raw damage when exposed and zero damage inside a pylon zone. It is intentionally devastating at the current 140-health proof baseline, but it is finite and continues through the standard armor/ward damage path.
- The Court owns protection geometry and Descent damage. The Examiner owns body state and timing. The encounter director owns camera, dialogue, music mix, and player cinematic lock. Presentation never decides protection or damage.
- Phase II increases the Examiner's movement cadence modestly and restores one encounter-owned music foundation after the impact. Independently composed complete songs cannot be treated as music stems; the rejected orchestral audition remains archived until one coherent vocal-orchestral composition or compatible authored stems replace the proof track.
- The debug timing proof uses 0.64 seconds of readable compression, a 0.17-second launch completely beyond the upper court edge, a 3.8-second absence, a 0.15-second meteor fall, 0.32 seconds of held contact, and 0.90 seconds of recovery. Body contact emits impact VFX, SFX, damage, and the 0.055-second black-silhouette/red-eye accent together.
- A future later-game rematch around Stage XV may damage or disable cover, but that escalation is documentation-only and cannot be backported into the Stage VII teaching encounter without a new decision.

## Consequences

- Stage VII can teach `read charge -> identify cover -> move -> survive` with exact, testable rules.
- The same four pylon coordinates drive art and gameplay, preventing false safe zones.
- Examiner, Executioner, and The One Above retain distinct ceilings: exact trial, overwhelming punishment, and reality-scale authority respectively.
- Divine damage/resistance/armor taxonomy remains future design work; the proof uses the current shared damage model instead of inventing a premature equipment system.
