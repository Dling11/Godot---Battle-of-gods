# Decision 122: Scope Forest Naming Without Globalizing It

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-22

## Context

Stages I-X are the planned Forest/root/tree regional arc, including later accessory and relic milestones. The problem is not the regional concept; it is repeatedly applying root/tree vocabulary to unrelated characters, global Sanctuary services, generic systems, or later regions until the opening biome appears to be the identity of Battle of Gods.

## Alternatives

1. Require most Stages I-X enemies, materials, NPCs, and systems to use Forest/root/tree names for consistency.
2. Remove the Forest identity after Stage V.
3. Retain Forest Stages I-X, but use the motif only where it meaningfully describes a regional creature, material, place, item, or specialist. Give unrelated characters and global systems their own personal or neutral identities.

## Decision

Choose Alternative 3.

- Stages I-X remain the planned Forest region. Existing enemies, materials, equipment, scenery, Nema's regional title/workshop, stable IDs, replay content, and planned accessory/relic cadence remain valid.
- Forest/root/tree/bark/wood/grove/mire/thorn/fungal vocabulary may be used when the subject genuinely belongs to that ecology. The regional setting does not require every new name to use those words.
- New Sanctuary characters and cross-region services receive simple personal names or names with intentional linguistic/lore meaning. They must not automatically combine the current region's motif with a profession.
- The approved blue witch is an unrelated transmutation specialist, not another Forest/root-category character. Her final personal name should reflect her own blue, sky, sea, magical, or personal lore.
- Generic authorities and interface labels use neutral functional language such as craft, exchange, transmute, memory, essence, crucible, material, or recipe.
- Stage XI+ regions establish their own naming language instead of inheriting the Forest motif.
- Historical ADRs, stable resource IDs, and implemented regional names remain unchanged.

## Consequences

- Stage VI-X can continue the Forest arc while introducing varied identities beyond repetitive root/tree naming.
- Nema remains Rootweaver Nema because her implemented role is specifically tied to the Forest ecology; this is not a template for naming every new service NPC.
- The future blue-witch source remains under a neutral path until her personal name is approved.
- Content reviews must ask whether a motif word adds real identity or merely repeats the current region.
