# Decision169: Renew Rootbound Hollow and preview enemy pressure

- Date:2026-10-03
- Status: Stage III environment/audio and optional pressure review implemented; normal balance preserved. Fresh enemy concepts recommended, not implemented.
- Source: Owner requested proceeding with the next biome slice, plus new enemy/mechanic recommendations and a question about higher spawn pressure.

## Decision

Continue Decision168's distinct-biome delivery in normal Stage III. Preserve west-side restrained corruption, living center/east, ritual seal,20px navigation clearance, narrative dialogue, solo Husk, F Reliquary and normal progression/save authority. Renew the32px authored ground and eight compact prop families; reuse composition-based canopy/wind/birds and scene-owned outdoor audio with separate combat cues.

Keep normal Stage III at10 Rootlings/four live. Offer14/five as an explicit no-save review using a duplicated approach resource before encounter start; leave the solo boss intact. Feedback about crowd pressure is not automatic approval to rebalance all six stages.

Recommend Rotcaller first (interruptible snapshotted root zone), then Burrowkin (visible emergence warning and punish window). Generate concepts and specify bounded mechanics without pretending to have implemented actors, animation or Hunt rewards. New actors should use generic spawn entries and separate authority/presentation.

## Alternatives and consequences

Increasing all live caps would hide the cause of readability/balance changes; retaining the narrative solo-boss gate gives a controlled comparison. Filling Stage III with uniform purple terrain would contradict its limited western incursion and erase the stage-to-stage ecological progression. A concept board is useful for owner review, but cannot substitute for real directional walking/attack/recovery animation or damage authority.

The normal environment is replaced with recoverable prior scene/layout/test snapshots. Shared coarse assets remain available for unrenewed later stages. Owner visual/listening review, full vulnerable combat and phone profiling remain open.
