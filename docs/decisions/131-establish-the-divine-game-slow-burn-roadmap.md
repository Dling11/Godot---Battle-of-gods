# Decision 131: Establish the Divine Game Slow-Burn Roadmap

## Status

Accepted — 2026-08-24

Decision 132 subsequently resolves the two Disciples' high-level visual, weapon, scale, and reusable-asset direction while leaving the Stage VII dialogue/reward/replay/flow contract open. Decision 133 keeps the ordinary Stage VII route compact but gives the optional white-gold Challenge branch a separate one-screen divine arena and the Examiner's restrained Axiom Divide: First Measure direction.

## Context

The existing story established King as an isekai father seeking his family's souls and described gods as consumers or spectators of mortal emotion. It also described The One Above as reality's primordial creator and strongest known being. That prematurely fixed a cosmic hierarchy and conflicted with the newer direction: the stages belong to a created divine Game within a larger universe, the apparent benefactor secretly helped manufacture King's motivation, and the truth should emerge slowly through Disciples, Champions, and contradictions.

Stage VII also needed a durable narrative function before character or environment production, while Stage IX's magical ruins and Stage XX's direct god encounter needed to remain roadmap-only.

## Alternatives Considered

1. Keep The One Above as confirmed creator of reality and strongest being, and treat the Game as the universe itself.
2. Reveal immediately that the gods and The One Above caused King's suffering.
3. Separate the Game from the universe, make The One Above its architect without locking the final hierarchy, and reveal his involvement through a slow-burn Champion story.

## Decision

Choose option 3.

- The Game is a constructed system within reality. It contains Champions, stages, trials, Sanctuary access, resurrection, monsters, rewards, materials, progression, and divine interference.
- The One Above architected the Game. The title does not establish that he created reality, created every god, or is the strongest being. `Aethron` remains an unapproved working-name possibility only.
- King was selected as a Champion and enters willingly after an unknown `???` offers hope that his family's souls remain reachable.
- Internally, The One Above selected King before or during the tragedy and materially helped create or exploit the loss that motivates him. The exact mechanism and the family's present condition remain open.
- Early story presents The One Above as rescuer and benefactor. The player must not be told immediately that the gods are the underlying threat.
- Stage VII becomes a compact story stage centered on one calm humanoid Disciple, an honest Continue/Challenge choice, and a mechanically superior but fair recognition test that does not require the Disciple's death.
- A contrasting second Disciple may be foreshadowed but is not introduced or named at Stage VII.
- Stage IX holds the modular reclaimed magical-ruins direction plus provisional fast-Mage and space-controlling Stone-Warden roles tied to future accessory materials.
- Stage XX is the planned first direct confrontation with The One Above. His impossible knowledge begins cracking the benefactor story without requiring the full truth to be revealed there.
- No rigid god hierarchy is established. Other gods and their Champions remain future world-building potential.
- Reusable content is organized by identity and role rather than introduction stage. Frame-authored important enemies use `AnimatedSprite2D`; physical action must read before VFX.

The detailed internal spoiler and disclosure contract lives in `docs/design/divine-game-story-roadmap.md`.

## Consequences

- Prior claims that The One Above created reality or is the strongest known being are superseded.
- King's family tragedy now has an intentional long-term causal mystery rather than functioning only as backstory.
- The Stage VII Disciple strengthens early trust in The One Above while giving the player an optional skill/power-gap test.
- Stage IX and Stage XX have clear narrative purposes without becoming implementation commitments.
- The opening will eventually require a separately approved revision; the current title-to-Sanctuary flow remains runtime truth.
- Stage VI still requires measured late-wave composition testing before its final balance is locked.
- Existing stage-numbered reusable asset paths remain runtime truth until a controlled reference-safe migration is approved; new reusable assets must follow identity-owned naming immediately.
