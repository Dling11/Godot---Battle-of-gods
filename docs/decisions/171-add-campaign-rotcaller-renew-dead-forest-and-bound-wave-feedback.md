# Decision171: Campaign Rotcaller, Dead Forest and bounded wave feedback

- Date:2026-10-03
- Status: Implemented; owner animation/mix/balance and phone profiling remain open.
- Context: Owner accepted the caster's identity but requested movement/outline polish, confirmed Stage III beside Rootlings with Husk retained, requested IV/possibly V and more hordes, distinct expedition music, Skill1 crowd-stutter correction and documented work after V.

## Decision

Use one caster per mixed wave and more queued enemies: III13+1/five live, IV60/eight live, V one finite six/five sentry encounter before unchanged Varkuun. Preserve boss/chest/save authority; guard completion never marks/banks the boss stage. Reward the campaign caster through the existing one-shot observer; remove it in the isolated trial.

Renew normal V with compact generated dead-forest props, new ash-soil32px terrain, coherent edges/open basin, separate gateway pillars and sparse wind/creaks. Keep distinct biome progression and legacy boss-proof sources. Add licensed CC0 expedition recordings through the existing single music owner; retain Sanctuary and Varkuun compositions.

Give Earthsplitter's released presentation ownership of bounded shared impacts. Per-target feedback remains; repeated global contact pauses do not. Use player-specific presenter lookup and reuse physics queries without dropping geometry checks. Preserve damage/reach/timing and other attack/incoming hitstop.

## Alternatives and consequences

- Replacing Husk or adding casters throughout Varkuun's root-prison fight would obscure the established boss lesson. Finite pre-boss guards provide variety without overlapping warnings indefinitely.
- Increasing the live cap aggressively or spawning endless mobs could amplify navigation/VFX costs and distort rewards. Bounded queues add pressure and actual XP while respecting existing caps.
- Frame-based hit coalescing fails for a travelling attack; weakening damage or hiding target feedback would mask it. The eight-target rendered reproduction measured six global pauses/369.85ms before and zero after, with all hits retained.
- Procedural placeholders were not substituted for requested art. Generated originals and exact prompts remain in the workspace; technical derivatives retain pixel scale/alpha/foot baselines.

After V, prioritize loading/start, portal/summon, contact audio, pointer/movement and measured frame profiling. VI, Burrowkin, scout/reactions/exploration/Hunts remain follow-ups. Automated proof does not establish enjoyment, final difficulty, musical seam quality or phone performance.
