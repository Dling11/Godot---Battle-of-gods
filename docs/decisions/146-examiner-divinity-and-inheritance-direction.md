# 146 — Examiner divinity and inheritance direction

Date: 2026-09-13. Status: Examiner identity and subsequent female Forest Goddess direction accepted; exact boss traits and inheritance details proposed; no runtime changes.

## Context

The owner clarified that Examiner is considered a god while still being a disciple. They also proposed a God of Plants at Stage X and rewards that can grant active skills or passives for resurrection, invulnerability, barriers, durability, damage and spell-focused play.

## Decision

Record Examiner as a divine being whose Disciple role describes service to a higher power. Preserve his required Stage XX placement, approved body identity and false-mentor narrative. Divinity, organizational role and encounter difficulty are distinct.

Expand design exploration to active and passive divine inheritances. Treat a true Plant God at Stage X as a candidate, superseding any assumption that only a beast/guardian is eligible there. Ten equipped active skills remains accepted under Decision 145; the proposed three divine-passive slots are not yet a fixed requirement.

The [inheritance proposal](../design/divine-inheritance-and-plant-god.md) records candidate encounters, powers and survival distinctions. It does not implement rewards or settle the family's ending.

## Alternatives

### Owner follow-up — Forest Goddess and boss traits

The owner accepts the Forest God direction for Stage X and specifies an attractive adult female plant/forest deity, Examiner-sized or larger. Growth and Renewal is the proposed domain wording; personal name, exact scale, mechanics and rewards remain open. The owner also requests distinctive passive boss traits, including Examiner. The inheritance design now proposes Living Dominion (root-fed finite bark protection) and Radiant Pressure (warned avoidable heat pulses). These are design candidates, not newly installed combat systems.

Treating Disciple as synonymous with non-divine contradicts the clarification. Treating every god as equally strong would unnecessarily constrain progression. Turning every inherited power into a new active button would miss the requested passive/build variety.

## Consequences

The Plant God, divine passive loadout, revival rules and reward mastery require later gameplay design and implementation. Stage X is not playable; Examiner's current F7 outcome remains rewardless. The family's death remains established, and any Champion-anchor explanation for player revival is still a proposal.
