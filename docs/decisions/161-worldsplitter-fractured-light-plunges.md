# 161 — Fractured-light impacts and repeated embedded plunges

Date: 2026-09-27. Status: superseded by Decision 162 after owner correction. Repeated strikes must summon fresh swords; the single-blade pullout below was rejected.

## Context and decision

The owner rejected the large stone bed and upgrades expressed mostly through size. They approved shattered light as the impact material, and specifically replaced the proposed Mature triangle with three rapid consecutive strikes into the same point. The sword must visibly bury, pull free, strike again and hold its final contact before dissolution.

Keep one short King command and world-fixed autonomous authority. Foundation has one blue-white plunge; Advanced has two, changing from blue-white to crimson during extraction; Mature uses one crimson sword for three consecutive plunges. All contacts share the chosen point, rather than splitting into a triangle or spawning three simultaneous effects. Dedicated extraction/restrike drawings and ground-plane clipping supply the buried contact. Rank does not enlarge the sword.

Replace the stone eruption with a small compression, low fractured-light shards and a fading scar. Reuse the outlined blade's formation/dissolution and add new generated impact/extraction atlases. Bound early sound/camera response; final contact is strongest. Keep the player model, Skill 4, .32s commitment, 20s cooldown, Eira Level 10/15 gates and compatible saved rank.

## Consequences

This supersedes Decision 160's two-contact stone eruption and scale-based rank presentation. Damage budgets remain 520/600/700% weapon damage, distributed across 1/2/3 contacts; final radii become 88/96/104px to match the compact effect. Only the last contact carries the existing .5s authored stun. Early contact push is light so the sequence does not immediately expel its targets. Initial contact is near .60s; repeats are .36s apart. Mature contacts therefore span .72s. Final embed holds .20/.24/.28s by rank before dissolution.

Rejected alternatives: oversized boulders; three static sword images; triangle placement; repeating full-strength former damage budgets; extra player lock for each plunge. Detailed rules, assets and verification: `docs/design/worldsplitter-fractured-light.md`.
