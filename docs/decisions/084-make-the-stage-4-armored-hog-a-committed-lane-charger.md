# 084 - Make the Stage 4 Armored Hog a committed-lane charger

## Status

Accepted and implemented on 2026-08-13.

## Decision

Stage 4 introduces one stylized Armored Hog as a dodge-and-punish crowd threat. It snapshots a straight charge during an obvious brace, resists frontal damage only while braced or charging, then becomes dazed after its committed run. Stage 4 fields at most two hogs in a wave while retaining its existing eight-live cap.

The enemy drops protected Armored Hog Hide and Living Bark Plate rolls. Its presentation uses every approved locomotion, charge, hurt, daze, collapse, and death frame. Original action sounds are layered with a CC0 boar vocal; no sound from the cited commercial inspiration is copied.

## Consequences

- Movement after the telegraph is the counterplay; raw frontal damage during the brace is intentionally inefficient.
- Charge direction is gameplay authority and presentation observes the snapshot.
- Stage 4 gains a new decision without increasing its already-approved total wave counts.
- The two new materials can be collected and persisted now, but crafting outputs remain future work.
