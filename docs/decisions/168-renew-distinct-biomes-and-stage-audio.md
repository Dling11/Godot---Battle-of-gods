# Decision168: Renew distinct biomes and stage audio

- Date:2026-10-03
- Status: Approved work program; Stage II art and Stage I/II outdoor audio/shared music handoff implemented. Remaining world-life slices planned.
- Source: Owner accepted all five recommendations, with emphasis on unique biome art and sound, and requested documentation plus immediate work.

## Decision

Use the accepted Stage I density/readability as a quality baseline while preserving each established biome: living I/II, corrupted III, eastward rot IV, dead V, contrasting teal/pale-stone ascent VI. Renew modular art/layout in stages. Stage II is the first delivery: distinct birch/thorn props,32px baked ground over unchanged world bounds, compact physical bases and clear ranged lanes.

Improve existing AudioDirector through a cancelable single-player fade-out/switch/fade-in. Reuse the attributed existing music compositions; do not overlap independently composed complete tracks. Add scene-owned data-configured outdoor recordings with sparse positional cues on Ambience -> Music and event-driven combat reduction. Gameplay/reward/save authority remains unchanged.

The recurring field scout, Sanctuary milestone reactions, small authored exploration and replayable Hunts are approved direction. They still require concrete playable slices, real animation/interaction, bounded rewards and normal save integration. Do not label them completed because the owner approved their inclusion.

## Alternatives and consequences

Replacing every stage with the Grove kit would erase the established ecological journey. A single painted backdrop would lose modular depth/obstacle control. Two-player crossfading full songs would complicate current ownership and briefly overlap incompatible compositions. A short fade handoff retains one complete music foundation; same-track requests keep continuity. Recorded CC0 nature sounds make an immediate listenable slice while a newly arranged regional score remains future work.

Profiles/scene owners are reusable for later biomes, but recordings, placement, collision and music balance must be reviewed with actual encounters. New art is active in normal Stage II, and its prior scene/layout/test is archived recoverably. Phone performance and final economy are separate follow-ups.
