# Decision 093: Author Stage 5 Around a Dormant Broken Shrine

## Status

Accepted — 2026-08-14; progression-destination and rejected-stump portions superseded by Decision 094

## Context

The Stage 5 boss combat proof had matured inside a flat test arena, but the planned entrance, dialogue, and encounter framing had no real environment to land in. Stage 5 must read as the forest's fully decayed destination after Stage 4's eastern rot, remain clear during rapid jump and root-prison attacks, and leave room for a later anonymous-power story event without presenting unfinished lore as a working portal.

## Alternatives

- Finish the boss entrance and audio inside the flat test arena, then rebuild their timing in the production map.
- Fill Stage 5 procedurally with random dead trees and reused Rootbound terrain.
- Make the northern landmark an active portal or god manifestation immediately.
- Author a deterministic boss basin first, with a dormant landmark whose later narrative use remains open.

## Decision

The F8 Stage 5 proof uses one authored 15-by-9 boss basin backed by a dedicated four-by-four, 64-pixel full-decay terrain atlas. Its center stays quiet and readable, stone/root bands create deliberate lanes, and denser root damage frames the perimeter. Four genuinely dead colliding trees define the arena corners without random placement. A broken forest shrine anchors the north edge with separate collision masses and an open center.

The broken shrine is dormant scenery only. It is not itself a `StagePortal`, has no destination, and does not yet trigger the proposed anonymous godlike presence. Its architecture also becomes the destination-side form of the damaged eastern gateway foreshadowed in Stage 4. Stage 4's clear ring activates inside that matching ruin, establishing one ancient route network without giving scenery transition authority. Its composition deliberately preserves a future presentation seam for an approved post-boss or Stage 6 event.

Stage 4's post-clear ring continues to return to Sanctuary until the real Stage 5 route, rewards, save authority, and transition contract are implemented. Changing that destination later must not require redesigning the shared gateway landmark. The F8 scene remains a combat-and-environment proof rather than normal progression.

## Consequences

- The boss entrance, landing, dialogue, and music can now be tuned against their intended composition once instead of against a disposable flat room.
- Stage 5 has distinct fully decayed terrain and dead-tree silhouettes rather than a random or recolored Stage 4 arrangement.
- Collision, navigation cutouts, depth, and occlusion remain part of every large prop's authored contract.
- The landmark creates visual anticipation without falsely promising an implemented portal or locking the later story event too early.
- Stage 4 and Stage 5 now share one recognizable broken-gateway family while their level scenes retain explicit destination authority.
- Decision 094 later supplies the production Stage 5 flow, removes the rejected stump family, and connects Stage 4 to it. The entrance package, dialogue, dedicated audio, final reward resolution, and possible godlike event remain separate work.
