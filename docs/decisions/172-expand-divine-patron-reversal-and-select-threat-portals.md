# Decision172: Divine patron reversal and the complete threat portal family

Date:2026-10-03.
Status: Owner-directed story expansion and portal concept selection documented; additional mechanisms proposed; no runtime implementation.

## Context

The owner selects all six latest portal concepts and asks for a story in which monsters/demons seem to invade, divine allies grant powers, and the apparent protectors become late-game enemies. Existing Decisions131,138 and146 already establish the constructed Game, credible early benefactor, divine false mentor, manipulation and proposed inheritances.

## Alternatives

1. Make all gods evil and all monsters innocent, immediately explaining the reversal.
2. Treat all-six selection as six new playable tiers and rewrite current campaign factions.
3. Preserve current canon and threat meanings, expand the gradual patron reversal, and keep exact monster origins, rewards and ending open.

## Decision

Choose option3. Record apparent invasion→credible divine aid→earned power→contradictory evidence→late conflict with responsible patrons. Not every god shares one motive and not every hostile monster is a victim. Gifts can remain genuine even when a patron's explanation is false.

Select all six concept designs as the desired art family: Normal blue, Mini Boss purple, red Boss hand and maw variants, future God white-gold and future Transcendent near-black/violet. These are five threat tiers with two authored red variants. Decisions119/129 retain destination-based assignment, F travel and chest/save order. Native assets, motion, integration and final in-engine review remain pending.

The family remains dead, the Stage100 promise remains manipulation, Examiner remains the planned divine false mentor at StageXX, and the direct One Above encounter/ending remain unscheduled/open. The One Above is not confirmed creator of reality or strongest being.

## Consequences

The [internal design expansion](../design/divine-patrons-and-manufactured-invasion.md) develops proposed monster origins, breach mechanisms, fair clues, genuine aid, gift autonomy and disclosure boundaries. It is not player-facing copy. Generic loading and title artwork must not spoil the reversal.

Earlier “portal choice pending” and P1/P2/P3 selection requests are superseded by the all-six selection. Concept approval does not pretend static boards are finished animation assets, add damaging gate hands or implement new gods/powers/monsters.
