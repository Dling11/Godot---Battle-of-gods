# Decision170: Renew Eastern Rot and prototype Rotcaller

- Date:2026-10-03
- Status: Stage IV environment/audio and isolated Rotcaller combat trial implemented; owner visual/mix/balance review remains open.
- Source: Owner authorized the recommended next slice: Eastern Rot renewal, then an interruptible caster tested with two Rootlings before broader roster pressure.

## Decision

Promote the distinct Stage IV art/layout into normal play, preserving its five waves,50 enemies, eight-live ceiling, Hog lessons, rewards/story and F route to Stage V. Preserve the living west/eastern36.90% incursion with32px terrain, compact generated prop families, rooted canopy presentation and regional recorded audio. Keep gateway pillars as separate navigation bodies so the opening stays usable.

Implement Rotcaller in a dedicated no-save review on the renewed map: real directional movement,1.1s fixed-position32px warning, one10-damage eruption, accepted-hit interruption, .8s recovery and5s cadence. Separate authority from drawing/animation/audio and compose existing combat/navigation components. Use a generic scene/count spawn entry and normal wave-slot accounting. Remove reward observers before readiness; override trial completion to exclude story/loot/portal progression. Default to one caster plus two Rootlings; optional Spitter adds one review slot.

## Alternatives and consequences

Raising campaign counts or inserting the new caster immediately would combine art, role and balance changes before the counterplay is readable. A static concept cannot prove walking/interrupt behavior; the dedicated trial exposes real sword contact and warnings with a vulnerable King. Preserving normal Stage IV resources also keeps Hog charge lessons independently testable.

Two scene-owned positional beds implement the ecological sound transition using existing attributed recordings, rather than stacking complete musical tracks. A future score or Hunt reward contract is separate work. Stage V/VI renewal, Burrowkin, field scout/reactions, exploration and Hunts remain planned. Owner acceptance and phone profiling are not inferred from automated checks.
