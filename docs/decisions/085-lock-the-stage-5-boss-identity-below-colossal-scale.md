# 085 - Lock the Stage 5 boss identity below colossal scale

## Status

Accepted on 2026-08-14.

## Context

Stage 5 needs the first major Forest boss to read clearly above the Stage 3 Rootbound Husk mini-boss without spending the game's later scale escalation. Stage 10 remains reserved for the first truly colossal or screen-dominating boss.

## Decision

Approve the generated massive hunched humanoid forest guardian identity: asymmetric root arms, heavy mobile legs, a split dead-bark torso shell, an internal luminous core, and armor that can visibly break away.

Normalize the future runtime body to approximately 1.35-1.5 times the Husk's standing height and 2.5-3 times King's visual mass. The boss remains fully visible in normal gameplay and leaves room to dodge. The concept's generated crowned scale figure is rejected as a King reference and has no asset authority.

## Consequences

- Stage 5 gains a visibly larger major boss without becoming a kaiju encounter.
- Armor break and exposed-core phase language arise naturally from the approved anatomy.
- A true runtime-scale standing proof using the actual King and Husk must pass before animation-sheet generation.
- Mechanics, frame budgets, name, reward, and Stage 5 encounter structure remain separate decisions.
