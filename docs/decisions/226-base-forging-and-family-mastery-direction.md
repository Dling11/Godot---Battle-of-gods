# 226 — Base forging, family mastery and combat expansion

Date: 2026-10-17. Status: owner direction accepted; detailed earning, NPC, visual milestones and tuning remain proposed. Not implemented by this decision.

## Direction

The owner requires weapon crafting at the Sanctuary/base, weapon techniques earned through mastery, and a weapon-upgrade concept with visible design/icon/UI milestones. Mastery5 is an example milestone. Retain physical/magic/pure damage, criticals and defense, and plan HP scaling plus abnormal statuses including poison/blind/miss/evasion alongside existing stun/knockback.

Keep King level, learned weapon-family mastery and particular forged-item upgrade tier separate. Exact mastery rules are not settled. The owner's possible new Sanctuary mentor is not an approved NPC identity/artwork yet.

## Earlier recommendation, unselected

Combat contribution supplies steady family XP, stage clears provide a bounded completion bonus, and mentor proofs unlock important lessons/evolutions. Campaign progress opens material/recipe/trial access. A family keeps its learning when a different item of that family is equipped; raw hit count does not determine XP.

Proposed new mentor: Veyr, the Godscarred, a mature mortal weapons master. Proposed division: Eira for King powers, Nema for forging, new mentor for weapon mastery/trials, Orren for existing preparation/workshop. None of this removes current NPC services or installs a character.

## Consequences

Implement a real family progression authority before showing earned HUD mastery. Validate the new base-only permission in crafting authority as well as UI. Preserve existing IDs, starting gear, learned skills, saves and accepted actor art. Prototype a small usable loop before six techniques across every family or a universal status matrix.

Damage types and critical/control authority already exist under178; general poison/blind/HP-scaling/evasion systems are still future work. New rules must respect mitigation, shields/invulnerability, boss control limits and explicit percent-HP caps. Selected C2 layout and skills-only bottom anchoring remain225.
