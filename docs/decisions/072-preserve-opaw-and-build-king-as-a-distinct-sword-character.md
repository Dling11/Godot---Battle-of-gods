# Decision 072: Preserve Opaw and Build King as a Distinct Sword Character

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-02
- **Amends:** Decision 071's Opaw replacement/archive consequence

## Context

Decision 071 approved character-owned combat, King, real combos, cinematic skills, and essence/relic equipment. Its first migration plan treated Opaw as a runtime predecessor that would be archived after King replaced him. The owner wants to keep Opaw, his identity, and his skills as playable content while still making King feel like a major new combat feature.

King should look young and in his physical prime despite being a father in his former life. He begins with a sword and uses the existing four-slot skill-control grammar, but his attacks, effects, timings, and skills must not be reskinned Opaw content.

## Decision

Opaw remains an active supported playable character. Preserve his compact-armless presentation, Ashwood/Iron runtime compatibility, Piercing Rush, Consecutive Thrust, preserved Sweeping Cut, progression data, audio, tests, and story identity. Move only rejected or genuinely superseded experiments to the archive; do not archive the active Opaw package after King launches.

King becomes a separate young-prime sword character and the planned narrative lead. He owns a character-visible signature sword, a fast buffered tap combo, a hold-to-charge heavy branch, four distinct active skills, larger honest contact areas, and stronger tiered impact presentation. He shares generic input, movement, dash, health, damage, ability, UI, inventory, save, loot, and stage systems with Opaw, but not Opaw's body frames, sword orbit, skill assets, or combat definitions.

The roster/save migration therefore adds characters rather than replacing Opaw. Existing Opaw saves stay valid; a future schema adds unlocked/selected character records and King's per-character progression without relabeling Opaw's existing skills as King content.

## Consequences

- Opaw requires an enduring character definition and regression coverage rather than a cutover archive package.
- King can use the same four skill slots and input devices while owning entirely new definitions and presentation.
- The character menu eventually needs roster selection and per-character equipment/loadout presentation.
- Existing Opaw story IDs remain valid historical facts. King needs separate stable story and awakening IDs.
- Shared profile state may include story, coins, materials, recipes, and unlocked characters; level, health, skills, mastery, and equipped essences remain per character.
- King art production starts with a small turnaround and attack proof. It must not recolor, resize, or trace Opaw frames.
