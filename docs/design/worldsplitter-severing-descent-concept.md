# Skill 5 concept revision — Severing Descent

Status: superseded/rejected 2026-09-27. The owner clarified that Worldsplitter must remain an upright straight-down attack; see `worldsplitter-upright-descent.md` for the implemented replacement. Historical proposal follows. The owner rejected the current reused Skill 5 composition and its X effects, requested fresh good frames and a somewhat larger effect, and accepted Skill 4 for now. This document does not promote the existing X prototype or claim the new motion is implemented.

## Concept

King makes a short commanding greatsword cut. A larger spectral sword forms over the chosen point, winds back, and performs one accelerating overhand chop. Blade-first impact briefly compresses the ground, then releases one broad connected eruption of pressure and slate fragments. The sword dissolves immediately after delivering its force. Empty space reappears quickly as debris settles.

The blade must change orientation through a clear swing; a static upright sword enlarged or translated down is not sufficient. Keep the luminous blade family of Skills 1/2 as a style reference, but draw this action and its eruption anew. Earthsplitter inspires the compact, legible debris clusters and impact timing; its traveling rupture strips are not recycled into another four-way pattern.

## Gameplay purpose

A targeted group finisher: place one decisive broad hit on clustered enemies while retaining personal mobility. Skill 1 remains lane pressure, Skill 2 local interruption, Skill 3 repositioning and Skill 4 preparation/protection. The fifth skill concentrates its damage in one meaningful contact instead of requiring crossed fracture lanes or a second planted cast.

Proposed timing: a short brace/command returns King to control around .28–.32s; the released blade lands around .34–.40s, then the dust/light settles over another .4–.5s. The targeting location commits at release and does not chase enemies. Damage timing, core/rim values, stun policy and cooldown must be measured separately before runtime promotion; do not grant extra hits because the eruption has several frames. Preserve existing equipment/level modifiers, target LOS, slot5 ownership and all Skill4 behavior.

## Motion and scale contract

- Eight concept key poses: gather, formation, backswing, accelerating descent, contact compression, peak eruption, clearing debris, settled aftermath.
- These are key poses, not a claim that eight drawings constitute the final smooth animation. Production target: roughly20–24 genuinely drawn states across sword and impact layers, with short contact hold and fast acceleration rather than equal-duration playback of a static blade.
- Increase effect silhouette roughly25–40% over the rejected pass for review; never resize King. Keep all blade tips and debris within padded cells.
- One broad eruption with a clear center and contained debris. No X/cross, four spokes, copied traveling trails, multiple swords, persistent luminous ring, or banana-shaped crescent.
- Presentation must stay independent of Player recovery. Rehearse cardinal aim, native viewport readability, source defeat/cancellation, terrain clipping and contact/damage alignment before delivery as a playable replacement.
- Audio plan: short airy gathering accent, accelerating blade rush, one strong grounded contact with a softer settling tail. One camera impulse at contact; no repeated shake from particles. No new audio has been authored for this concept pass.
