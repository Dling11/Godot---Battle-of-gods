# Divine inheritance and the Stage X Forest Goddess

2026-09-13. **Accepted direction:** Examiner is a god and a disciple serving a higher power at Stage XX; Stage X has a female Forest Goddess direction, Examiner-sized or larger. **Proposed:** exact boss traits, the inheritance system, passive slots, reward identities and build examples below. No new gameplay, encounter or reward is implemented here.

## Divine identity and allegiance

God describes what Examiner is; Disciple describes his allegiance and office. A god can serve another divine being. The title does not put every god at identical combat strength, nor establish The One Above as reality's creator or the strongest being.

The owner now proposes an actual God of Plants for Stage X. The earlier preference for a beast/guardian at that milestone is no longer the only candidate. A regional Plant God followed by the Stage XX divine Examiner is compatible with the hierarchy: their domains, authority, experience and encounter constraints differ.

Do not reinterpret the current Stage V boss Varkuun as this god or change Examiner's approved body identity. The playable campaign still ends at VI; both the Stage X candidate and production Stage XX integration remain future content.

## Proposed divine inheritance

A first major divine victory can grant a uniquely identified Domain Echo. King learns a recognizable part of the enemy's fighting style rather than copying its entire health pool, immunity or long boss animation.

An inheritance may contain:

- An **active technique** for the ten equipped slots.
- A **divine passive** that changes defense, survival, damage or spell behavior.
- Later **mastery choices** that evolve the inherited power without endlessly adding compulsory buttons.

Ten active skills on 1–0 are accepted. A separate allowance of **three equipped divine passives** is only a recommendation; the owner has not fixed its size. Innate King Resolve remains separate. Learned passives can exceed the equipped allowance, letting the player build for a boss without stacking every god's strongest defense simultaneously.

Powers are guaranteed first-clear/scenario rewards, not rare random drops. Some gods may be killed; others defeated, released or persuaded. The authored outcome owns the reward. Replays may grant bounded materials/mastery; they must not duplicate unique unlocks or create infinite permanent damage growth.

Acquiring a domain does not lock King into a class or alter his approved compact appearance. A magic-focused King, defensive King and aggressive greatsword King are loadouts using the same character.

## Stage X direction — Forest Goddess of Growth and Renewal

Owner follow-up accepts the Forest God direction and requests an attractive adult woman with strong plant/forest identity, at least Examiner-scale and potentially larger. Personal name and exact silhouette remain open; The First Bloom is a working title. No art or encounter has been produced for this direction.

Proposed appearance: an elegant readable face, long hair intertwined with leaves and flowers, a branching living crown, and layered petal/leaf garments with bark accents. Graceful deliberate gestures should contrast Examiner's rigid martial stance. Preserve the established top-down pixel density and palette discipline. Preview King, Examiner and her on one ground baseline; try a body about 15-25% taller than Examiner as an option, not a locked runtime measurement. Crown and foliage may extend the silhouette, but must not hide ground warnings or imply that every decorative branch is a collision surface. Use a stable reachable ground anchor and independently authored body hurtbox/movement footprint.

Working title, not a final name. A sacred figure suspended within a moving crown of roots and flowering branches could express both beauty and danger. Its gestures grow the arena, petals signal toxic blooms, and visible root veins carry life back to its heart.

The encounter could teach:

1. **Regrowth:** visible feeding roots restore a limited amount of health or renew a protective shell. Cutting them creates a clearly announced exposed-heart opening. Ordinary attacks can solve this; no mandatory fire skill is required.
2. **Claimed ground:** roots and poison flowers change which routes remain safe. Players can destroy dangerous growth or reposition instead of only waiting out a timer.
3. **Renewal:** a later phase rapidly blooms previously broken ground, previewing the life-preserving power King may eventually learn. Do not revive the boss indefinitely or erase all player progress.

Candidate inheritances:

| Power | Type | Purpose | Visual identity |
|---|---|---|---|
| Venom Bloom | Active | A targeted poisonous flower creates sustained area pressure | Seed, opening thorn petals, low vapor; no opaque screen-filling fog |
| Verdant Grasp | Alternate active | Roots briefly hold susceptible enemies in a chosen area | Ground cracks and ascending roots with a clear release |
| Living Bark | Passive | Periodically grows a finite defensive layer; the layer must be spent/broken before it can regrow | A restrained bark outline that visibly fractures on absorption |
| Second Spring | Advanced passive | Once per expedition, recover from ordinary lethal damage with partial health | King collapses, roots form a cocoon, the body reforms and the cocoon opens |

Recommendation: Stage X introduces one active and Living Bark. Second Spring is a later mastery reward, so the first plant encounter does not immediately grant every survival tool. Exact unlocks, health values, durations and passive capacity are open.

## Proposed signature boss traits

The owner requests unique persistent traits for gods, including Examiner. Exact mechanics below are proposals, not implemented damage auras, new immunities or barriers. Prefer one defining trait and a phase evolution per boss before adding more overlapping systems. Display a short trait explanation and a consistent visual tell; each trait needs an actionable response and must respect earned damage windows.

**Forest Goddess — Living Dominion:** a bounded set of visible feeding roots periodically restores a capped, finite bark barrier. Damage can break the barrier directly; severing the feeding roots stops replenishment for a meaningful window. No unlimited HP regeneration, reflected melee damage or required elemental skill. Visible light travels along each active root; bark plates crack as the barrier falls. This makes sustained arena control her identity. Second Spring at low HP can change where roots and flowers grow once, while preserving remaining HP and avoiding an indefinite resurrection loop. Root counts, barrier capacity and replenishment timing need encounter testing.

**Examiner — Radiant Pressure:** his existing aura could periodically gather into a visibly warned expanding ring of divine heat during ordinary combat. Only the moving ring edge deals one eligible hit per pulse; the cleared interior permits close combat. A normal well-timed dodge can cross it. Bounded range, interval and warning must accommodate King's starter movement and melee reach. This creates a recurring timing test instead of unavoidable continuous contact damage that rewards permanent retreat. At 35% HP, a paired pulse is a candidate only if the gap respects dodge recovery and overlapping attacks retain a legal escape.

Radiant Pressure must stop and clear when a seal charge begins, during earned seal-break stun/recovery, scripted transitions, defeat or encounter cleanup. It must not consume the player's damage window, inherit Verdict's dodge bypass, or turn the presentation aura itself into damage authority. Avoid adding a second general barrier to Examiner: his charge seal already provides a distinct defensive mechanic. Boss-specific control resistance should remain explicit; new blanket immunity is not implied by divinity.

Inherited powers should preserve a recognizable part of these traits with player-appropriate limits. Living Bark could reflect her finite protection; an optional later Examiner heat-pulse variant could reward a timed action rather than provide constant free area damage. Neither reward is approved for implementation yet.

## Candidate Stage XX — Examiner's inheritance

Examiner can give King a power while framing it as proof of worth. The reward appears credible and generous to King, reinforcing the false-mentor relationship. This does not establish that Examiner knows every detail of the lie or that the power has a hidden curse.

- **Facet Ward, active:** a short directional divine barrier with finite capacity. A properly timed use absorbs an eligible blow; only explicitly reflectable projectiles return to their source.
- **Borrowed Sun, alternate active:** a compact version of the Examiner's orb, released after a short player commitment and developed in flight or at its aimed impact. Do not transplant the boss's long charge lock into King.
- **Measured Will, passive candidate:** a brief, bounded anti-interruption window during selected committed techniques. It is super armor, not damage immunity; it does not turn every cast into a safe tanking action.

These are alternatives to review, not a commitment to grant all three together. Examiner's current F7 outro remains rewardless and his production reward flow is not implemented.

## Survival effects must mean different things

- **Barrier:** a finite damage pool or directional block, visibly breakable.
- **Damage reduction:** damage still reaches HP, reduced by a bounded amount; stacking cannot create accidental permanent immunity.
- **Super armor:** resists interruption while ordinary damage still applies.
- **Invulnerability:** a brief explicit interval; existing attacks marked as piercing normal invulnerability keep that rule.
- **Resurrection / Second Spring:** a saved, limited second chance after eligible HP loss, not constant healing. It cannot refresh through reloading, defeat retry or equipment swapping, recursively trigger another revival, or override an explicitly scripted scenario failure.

For a prototype, Second Spring would reset only at a genuine new-expedition boundary and use partial health plus a short recovery grace window. Its use must be persisted atomically with the recovery so loading an old transient state cannot duplicate the charge. Future multiplayer revival rules remain undefined.

Boss roots/stuns and generic player status effects still respect existing Light/Elite/Heavy/Boss authority. In particular, generic Shock cannot replace Examiner's earned seal-break stun.

## Resurrection and the family story

The family remains dead. The proposed player revival restores an eligible, still-anchored Champion inside the Game; it does not establish that a lost identity can be recreated or that the family's souls are present.

This distinction can support the manipulation: King experiences a real return from death and believes the gods' larger promise is possible. The gods exploit that conclusion without the narrative confirming it. The anchor mechanism itself remains a proposed explanation, not newly implemented lore or a chosen ending.

Do not resolve the family twist or remove earned powers automatically because the truth is discovered. Learning to wield a god's power on his own terms remains a possible late-game theme.

## Builds and pacing

A defensive loadout could combine a plant defensive passive with Facet Ward and a protection-domain effect. A spell-focused loadout could combine lightning, gravity, Venom Bloom and a bounded spell-echo passive. A duelist could favor Breakstep, precision damage and a controlled anti-interruption passive.

Each passive consumes a meaningful build allowance. Survival trades against offense/utility without secretly changing equipment rules. Exact stacking order, cooldowns and progression curves must be designed alongside existing armor/ward, equipment caps and stage ceilings.

Power growth should add new decisions and domain-specific strengths; it should not lengthen every attack animation or make every spell a large radial explosion. Short release times, legible enemy warnings and moving body frames remain requirements.
