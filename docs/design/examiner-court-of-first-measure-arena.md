## Visual Contract

- one-screen, true top-down/slightly angled top-down combat court with no horizon;
- a large circular playable field, broad open center, and clear southern entrance threshold;
- desaturated teal, blue-gray, and pale mineral stone that keeps King and the ivory Examiner readable;
- low-contrast concentric measurement rings, incomplete arcs, radial divisions, and abstract Split Glaive geometry;
- no dragon, serpent, skull, face, deity portrait, readable text, or permanent divine emblem before that emblem is separately approved;
- four symmetrical divine pylons outside the primary movement ring so presentation collision cannot obstruct dodging;
- small pale-cyan/blue-white pylon flames separated from the static architecture;
- restrained warm-gold inlays that remain quiet until combat presentation activates them;
- broken outer stone and shallow elevation that communicate a suspended divine place without introducing a side-view vista;
- no Forest trees, roots, ordinary ruins, throne, corpse, or clutter inside the challenge arena.

The floor engraving must remain quieter than every authoritative attack warning. During Axiom Divide, presentation may dim the dormant engraving while separate gold-white lane overlays illuminate. Static terrain must never be mistaken for an active hit area.

## Divine Descent — Phase Transition

At 70% health in the debug proof, the Examiner stops the fight, draws a short camera focus, and calmly acknowledges King before launching out of view. King regains control for 3.8 seconds while an original white-gold seal charges at arena center. The four cyan pylons expose 54-pixel protection zones driven by the exact same coordinates as damage authority. The center impact deals 260 finite raw damage when exposed and zero damage when protected, then leaves a 0.95-second recovery before Phase II.

Fresh frame-authored preparation, launch, fall, contact, and recovery remain readable without VFX. The generated seal is split into counter-rotating presentation layers; charge glow, safe-zone rings, debris, shockwaves, camera response, and five original cues enhance the action. A future Stage XV rematch may threaten cover, but Stage VII must keep all four zones reliable.

## Axiom Divide — First Measure

The Stage VII signature technique is the restrained test form of Axiom Divide. Restrained describes power and mechanical complexity, not a lack of spectacle.

Presentation sequence:

1. ambient combat sound pulls back and the Examiner becomes still;
2. his halo snaps into exact alignment and restrained particles draw inward;
3. the Split Glaive separates into three readable blade sections;
4. precise gold-white lane warnings appear over the arena independently of the floor art;
5. two separated sections perform sequential cuts;
6. the Examiner commits to the final marked lane with an explosive dash;
7. delayed cut afterimages resolve, the weapon reconnects, and one divine chime closes the sequence;
8. avoiding the complete measure may trigger a small head tilt and distinct recognition response.

The first encounter uses accurate, readable lanes with genuine safe space. A future `Final Measure` may add more independently moving blades and intersecting patterns, but it is not part of Stage VII production.
