# Worldsplitter: fractured light and meaningful rank choreography

**Superseded by implemented Decision161:** The owner approved fractured-light impacts and three consecutive same-point Mature plunges with burial/extraction. The triangle and former stone/two-contact progression below are historical. See `worldsplitter-fractured-light.md`.

Status: proposal only, 2026-09-27. No gameplay or art implementation in this pass.

## Owner feedback

The owner finds the ground-smashing effect excessive and the final upgrade too similar to enlarging the base version. They suggested red swords, repeated strikes or multiple swords as examples of meaningful upgrades, and have not chosen a new ground-effect design. The implemented straight descent, early control return and outlined blade remain the reference. The current enlarged stone field is not a settled visual direction.

## Recommended impact

Replace the rising boulder field with fractured light: a brief blade-tip compression, a low outward pressure ripple, a handful of clearly outlined angular light shards and a shallow dark impact scar with a narrow luminous edge. Keep the sword dominant. Warm-gray dust is a short secondary accent, not a lasting cloud. No crossed fissures, piles of rocks, opaque light dome or multiple simultaneous bright bursts.

Author contact, expansion, fragment flight and disappearance as distinct drawings; do not stretch the current stone eruption or recolor it and call it a redesign. Sample at native gameplay size. Preserve strong dark/light separation from Skill 1.

## Proposed rank choreography

| Rank | Distinct action | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation | One blue-white blade, one decisive impact | Concentrated aimed finisher |
| Advanced | Blue-white strike followed by a crimson second blade at the same point | Better focused damage and sustained pressure |
| Mature | Three sequential blades in a tight triangle: left, right, then a stronger crimson central finishing strike | Wider group coverage with a readable final hit |

The exact triangle spacing and hit radii must be previewed before implementation. All swords stay upright and drive straight down. Early effects clear as the next blade arrives; never pile three full explosions together. More blades are not an automatic multiplication of total damage: distribute a tuned rank damage budget across the contacts. Only the final designated heavy contact should carry stun, preserving enemy/boss control policy. A focused target should not receive three full copies of the former two-hit damage budget.

Keep one short King command with the sequence continuing after movement/dodge returns. Additional blades do not add player animation lock. Fix all strike locations at release rather than tracking enemies indefinitely. Existing Eira/save/rank ownership is the integration route; exact numbers, cadence, target marker footprint and training gates must be reviewed with the new choreography.

Audio: a distinct descending cue per blade, shorter lighter early contacts, and one deeper final impact. Bound shared camera feedback; strongest shake on the final hit. Existing audio is a reference, not a claim that this new sequence is authored.
