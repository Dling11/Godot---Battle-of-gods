# Skill 4 proposal: Unbroken Stand

Status: superseded concept history, 2026-09-27. The owner prefers a self-buff for Skill 4 and Worldsplitter in Skill 5; see `king-skill-4-unbroken-vow-proposal.md`. No Skill 4 gameplay, art, audio or slot resource has been changed. Worldsplitter remains playable in slot 4 until the replacement is ready.

## Why change it

Earthsplitter owns aimed lane pressure, Molten Crash owns fast close-area stun, and Sovereign Pursuit owns precise mobility. Current Worldsplitter calls down a giant sword for a 58px first hit and 104px delayed explosion at a target point, with a 1.63-second total windup/active/recovery commitment before hit pauses. It is powerful, but its second ground-impact sword repeats the visual and input language of Skills 1/2. Skill 4 has room to add a distinct defensive timing choice while remaining the strongest early King technique.

## Core play

King plants his real greatsword and takes a short, readable guard stance. The player can keep steering through the brief startup, then commits to a roughly 0.4-second counter window. One ordinary incoming attack during that window is heavily reduced and triggers a single immediate circular retaliation. The center punishes the attacker; a broader, weaker edge clears breathing room. Successful timing returns control soon after the contact. If nothing strikes King, he still finishes with a smaller push wave so the button has a useful fallback, but the strong center and stun reward are lost. The counter neither tracks an enemy forever nor blocks scripted boss verdicts that require their own sanctuary rule.

This gives Skill 4 a reason to use it when a crowd closes or a boss commits to a readable attack. Skill 3 can reposition King into a risky but valuable counter position; Skill 2 can hold enemies after the counter; Skill 1 can punish the resulting lane. Counter success is a timing reward, not a passive aura or a guaranteed safe escape. Existing boss stun resistance and explicit stun rules remain authoritative.

## First tuning targets, not approved balance

- Aim for 0.12-second startup, 0.4-second counter window, about 0.2-second recovery and an 18–20-second cooldown. Player control should return after the contact while ground aftermath finishes visually.
- Reduce one qualifying hit rather than grant continuous invulnerability. Cap both damage prevented and retaliatory damage; the move must not turn Examiner's 800-raw Verdict into a free counter or trivialize other encounter-specific break rules.
- On success, prototype a compact high-damage center and a larger low-damage push rim. The untriggered fallback should be useful against nearby crowds but much weaker than the successful counter. Test exact radii/damage against the current Worldsplitter and Stage VI crowd before locking numbers.
- Do not stack repeated counter triggers during one use. No automatic enemy pull, repeated damage ticks, or expanding persistent hazard.

## Presentation contract

Create new King C-consistent body drawings for planting the blade, holding the guard, taking the weight of an incoming blow and releasing the counter. Keep the accepted foot baseline, shoulder carry, outline, palette and logical scale. The visible impact starts exactly when the guard accepts a hit: a tight white-blue contact flash, compressed ground ring, then short outward pressure/debris frames with a clear safe visual center. A failed guard releases a visibly smaller ring. One restrained metal/body thud and low stone break support success; the fallback is quieter. Use one camera pulse at accepted contact, with no crowd-hit sound multiplication. Author enough frames for weight without lengthening the control lock.

## Review boundary

First prove the timing, damage authority, animation poses and readable success/fallback in F7 against normal enemies and a boss tell. Then decide whether Unbroken Stand replaces Worldsplitter in Skill 4. Preserve Worldsplitter as a future divine technique candidate until the owner chooses; no new skill is currently equippable from this proposal.
