## Accepted reusable foundation

The owner explicitly wants many future skills built from the approved separate-summon motion language. Preserve this sword while revising Earthsplitter ground presentation. The owner rejected the pressure-only revision as thin and boring. Current ground presentation restores the original substantial rock/dust eruption animation, kept upright and sequenced along a connected fracture bed, with white-blue bursts and a stronger finish. This preserves the splitter identity while adding energy; it does not add damage contacts. No banana/crescent projectile. A fixed 164px aiming lane rotates freely; only terrain reduces reach. Future milestone forms can increase bounded reach/width/damage without making cursor distance a power control or lengthening player commitment.

## Summoned motion and weight - latest owner clarification

- Skill 1: King initiates the action; a distinct large sword manifests into its windup, accelerates through an overhead swinging arc and strikes blade-first ahead of him. Its ground contact launches the advancing earth rupture. Keep V3's expressive swing and fracture direction, but replace its attached enlarged-blade treatment.
- Skill 2: the separate summoned sword rises briefly in anticipation, then accelerates downward into the nearby ground to produce the accepted local molten burst. King performs a coordinated cast/strike action and braces on contact.
- Both need authored weight: a readable short anticipation, faster travel into contact, a sharp impact beat, follow-through and smooth recovery. Scarf, dust and stone respond with delayed motion; debris follows short falling arcs and settles. Strong sound and camera feedback belong at actual contact. These are animation/feedback requirements, not a request for rigid-body weapon simulation or terrain deformation.
- Preserve quick player control return after release; the summon, traveling rupture and aftermath can continue independently. Exact timings and frames remain for the motion proof. Emotion comes from the full body and changing speed, not just effect size or prolonged casting.

## Skill 1 - Earthsplitter (working label)

Aim a point/direction and perform the coordinated action that summons a separate large sword into an overhead swing. Its blade-first ground contact ahead of King releases a fast advancing earth rupture toward the aim. The earliest form has a short single release; upgrades can add sequential rupture impacts and a final endpoint explosion. Counts and exact upgraded shapes remain provisional; the finisher should be the last impact, not an unannounced extra full-damage hit. Damage, range and width can rise at bounded milestones. No stun is proposed for this skill. The apparent floor destruction is presentation only at this stage, not authorization for terrain deformation or navigation changes.

Damage must be budgeted across the full cast rather than multiplying the original full hit by every new impact. King regains movement after the release; extra trail impacts must not lengthen his casting lock. Locked aim, exact per-impact collision/visual alignment, walls, repeated-target rules and progression gates need resolution during implementation. Additional stored casts may be a separate future upgrade; charges are not the same as impact count, and neither is implemented here.

## Skill 2 - Molten Crash (working label)

King performs a coordinated action as a separate heated white spirit sword appears, lifts briefly and crashes blade-first into the ground near his feet, creating the accepted circular molten explosion. This is a self-area attack, not a remote target spell. The initial crash can apply explicit stun through the existing target resistance rules. Upgrade candidates add damage, moderate area growth and eventually a finite heat aftershock on the same ground scar. Aftermath damage must not repeatedly stun. King can leave while the residual continues at its original location.

Stun eligibility is explicit, never inferred from damage. Molten power's lore/unlock source and any true burning status remain proposals; no god inheritance is granted by these samples. Stage caps, equipment/stat rules, ten equipped slots and the requirement for distinct progressing skills remain constraints.
